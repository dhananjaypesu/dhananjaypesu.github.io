About us

A reading circle within ten minutes' walk of every child.

Deepa started in 2019 as one volunteer, one solar lamp and a box of donated picture books on a doorstep in Lakshmi Nagar.

Many children in the settlements we work in are enrolled in school but still cannot read a short paragraph by Class 5. Their homes are small, evenings are noisy, and there is often no one free to sit with them and a book. A reading circle gives them a quiet hour, a trained mentor and a lending shelf.

Each circle has no more than 30 children and one mentor for every eight. Mentors are young people from the same neighbourhood, paid a monthly stipend and trained by us every Saturday. College volunteers join on weekends.

Read with them, not to them.

Programs

Three programs, one goal: a child who reads for pleasure.

Every program is free for families. We run them in community halls, temple courtyards and anganwadi rooms that neighbours lend us after hours.

Core program

Evening Circles

A 90-minute session five evenings a week. Children are grouped by reading level, not by age, and move up as they progress.

How a circle works

One evening, start to finish.

Every circle follows the same 90-minute routine, so children know what comes next and mentors can run it without notes.

  1. 6:00

    Lamp on

    Roll call, water, and five minutes to settle down.

  2. 6:15

    Sound games

    Letters and sounds through clapping and chalk games.

  3. 6:40

    Pair reading

    An older reader and a newer one share a levelled book.

  4. 7:10

    Story aloud

    The mentor reads, stops, and asks what happens next.

  5. 7:30

    Books home

    Book bags are checked out and the lamp goes off.

Impact

We measure every child on the reading ladder, twice a year.

The ladder has four rungs: letters, words, a short paragraph, and a full story. We test in June when children join and again in March.
